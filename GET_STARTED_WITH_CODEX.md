# Start or continue Obsidian-Bussines-Brain with Codex

[Español](PROMPT_INICIO_CODEX.md) · [Onboarding](docs/en/onboarding.md)

English guidance: [knowledge workflow](docs/en/knowledge-workflow.md) and [user guide](docs/en/user-guide.md). The operational skill files and `docs/FLUJO_EMPRESA_COMPLETA.md` / `docs/MARKETING_EMPRESARIAL.md` referenced in the prompt below are preserved Spanish operational references for the agent; their filenames are unchanged.

Install or continue the knowledge workspace for **my real business** using this repository and Obsidian. The result is a vault that organizes how we work and decide, turns repeatable criteria and patterns into instructions usable by people or agents, and evolves when I provide new conversations, files, customers, decisions, and work. Complete the full process; do not stop after one note or passing technical tests.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`; activate `ce-instalacion` only if no instance exists, otherwise `ce-continuidad`. Check the actual checkout, Obsidian, and vault. Honor permissions already granted; do not ask again. Do not create another vault or edit an existing one blindly.
2. Recover admissible original sources I can provide or that are within the authorized scope. Exclude writing I have rejected. Listen to my explanation: recover who does what, how decisions are made, signals, episodes, exceptions, alternatives, corrections, and why they matter. Ask only what changes a decision or document and is absent from the sources.
3. Activate `.agents/skills/ce-desarrollo-empresa/SKILL.md` and `docs/FLUJO_EMPRESA_COMPLETA.md` to cover the business by tasks and sources. Use `ce-arquitectura-conocimiento` and `ce-documentacion` where appropriate. For brand, market, demand, or content, also use `docs/MARKETING_EMPRESARIAL.md` and connect communication to customers, offers, delivery, and economics. Decide what belongs in D01–D07 syntheses or X01–X07 dossiers, which units need independent identity, which existing notes need revision, and how they connect. Do not create notes to meet a quota or narrate the business from outside. Write using its own vocabulary and mechanisms; keep sources and states in CONTROL.
4. Materialize the work in the authorized vault with stable IDs, sources, links, history, and backups when changing existing notes. Provisional capture is not the delivery. If I authorized development and correction, proceed without asking for every file; do ask about unresolved business decisions, editorial approvals, and external publication.
5. Execute a real task from the vault with `ce-consulta`: another person or agent should recover judgment, produce useful work, and recognize an exception. Also test a question that should retain uncertainty. If the output is generic, loses my judgment, or confuses a plan with a sale, trace source → extraction → note → retrieval and correct the failure before claiming usefulness.
6. Continue daily operation with `ce-continuidad` and `ce-cambios`. Review each new conversation or file for impact and improve relevant notes without repeating the initial interview or duplicating claims. Verify Obsidian, Bases, `estado`, `validar`, and actual use separately. Do not schedule recurring tasks or automatic memory without an explicit decision. Preview the connection map and publish it only with the required backup and authorization.

The repository contains software and synthetic examples; keep each instance's data outside the distributed product. Report what was actually installed, developed, and tested, what task the knowledge supports, and which genuinely missing fact or decision prevents a specific function. A note, report, or CI result does not prove that the complete knowledge system works.

When I identify new material as a transcript or conversation, follow the [default cycle (Spanish operational reference)](.agents/skills/ce-banco-transcripciones/references/flujo-predeterminado.md) through the integration authorized for my instance without requiring a request for every stage. Persist my configuration and scope privately for future sessions; do not inherit another business's permissions. For multipart deliveries, preserve each exactly and do not claim completeness before the verified ending. An ordinary document or long question retains its original task.
