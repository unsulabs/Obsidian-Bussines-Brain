## Preserved source and practical use

The complete vNext-1.0R methodology, catalog, prompt library, handover material and original research remain **Spanish references**, preserved at [metodologia/vNext-1.0R](../../metodologia/vNext-1.0R/). This bilingual guide explains how the product uses them; it is not a translation of the full source collection. The vNext-0.9 handover contract is a separate technical contract, not a competing methodology version.

Preserve the sequence P01 → K1/K2/K3/K4 → expert elicitation for K1 → G1–G7 → specialized document → audit. The detailed definitions and prompts belong to the original methodology. Gaps remain visible; neither a model nor a valid YAML header certifies business truth. Do not replace the sequence with a catalog of empty notes.

## Coverage by task and source

| Family | Work it should support | Evidence and useful check |
|---|---|---|
| D01 Direction | Choose opportunities and explain tradeoffs | Owner's dated criteria; compare two real opportunities. |
| D02 Market, customers and brand | Prepare a conversation or message for an observed situation | Customer voice, episodes and approved assets; no invented segments. |
| D03 Portfolio and commercial | Know what can be offered and quoted now | Current offer, authority and specific proposals; do not generalize a private price. |
| D04 Execution and quality | Perform and transfer work, including exceptions | Practitioner, process and case; test a task and exception. |
| D05 Economics and cash | Decide using supported costs and cash conditions | Accounting and transaction records; quotation is not collection. |
| D06 People and authority | Know who decides, performs and approves | Actual people, delegation and agreements; no fictitious departments. |
| D07 Risks and commitments | Respond to material obligations and dependencies | Contract, incident, provider and applicable sources; risk is not automatically obligation. |

X01–X07 provide specialized dossiers when needed. These families are perspectives, not a requirement to create seven files, seven departments or a fixed number of notes. Independent offers, processes, decisions, projects and research retain their own identity when history, permissions, scope or maintenance justify it.

The wider coverage map also asks about suppliers/resources, technology/data and learning. Ask which source governs each question and which task the answer will enable. Self-employed people and new ventures need the same clarity without assuming employees, a legal entity, a country, currency or tax regime.

## From knowledge to repeatable work

Start with one useful result: a proposal the owner can correct, an execution guide, a decision comparison or a source-grounded customer message. Review and use it, then preserve corrections and continuity. Installation and provisional capture alone do not finish a request to build the business.

Marketing draws on D02/D03 and remains connected to direction, delivery, economics and permissions. Retrieve audience evidence, positioning, offer status, claims and approved proof before drafting. An internal idea is not automatically a public promise. Optional recurring content recipes need an explicit audience, sources, approval and learning loop; they do not install scheduling or authorize publication.

Acceptance asks whether a person can perform the intended task and handle an exception, whether the writing preserves the expert's mechanism and reasoning, and whether unsupported commercial implications remain proposals. Editorial review and authorization remain separate from technical validation.
